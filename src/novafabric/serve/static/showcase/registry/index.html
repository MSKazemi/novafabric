The wow moment

Click code-review-prompt@0.2.0. See the failed edge-case-coverage eval suite. That's why v0.1.0 is still the production-promoted version. Eval-gated promotion in five seconds.

On your machine

nova register code-review-prompt-0.2.0.yaml
nova eval code-review-prompt@0.2.0
nova promote code-review-prompt@0.2.0
nova list --type prompt
