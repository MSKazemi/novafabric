# NovaFabric v0.87.0 — 2026-07-25

**Your GPAI documentation, sealed and change-tracked for a decade.** ADR-0107's GPAI
Art. 53 Model Documentation Form (NF-093) ships: keep the Art. 53(1) technical
documentation as a hash-chained revision history — every material change sealed and
diffable, each revision retained ten years. `experimental`. Full detail: `CHANGELOG.md`
§[0.87.0]. **This completes ADR-0107's pure-code exporter set.**

## Highlights

### The GPAI Art. 53 documentation form (ADR-0107 §NF-093)

Article 53(1) obliges general-purpose AI model providers to draw up, keep up to date, and
retain the model's technical documentation. NovaFabric keeps it as a **sealed revision
history**, not a mutable document:

- `build_gpai53_form(model_name, initial_fields=…, created_at=…)` seals the first
  revision. `append_revision(form, fields=…, created_at=…)` seals each subsequent material
  change.
- Every revision is **canonically hashed** (via the shared `novafabric._hashutil`, the
  same digest helper the Evidence Bundle uses) and carries its predecessor's digest in
  `prev_digest` — a hash chain over the whole change history.

### Tamper-evident material-change history

`verify_history(form)` recomputes every revision's digest and checks every predecessor
link. A silent edit to an already-sealed revision, or a broken `prev_digest`, makes it
return `False`. You can hand an auditor the form and they can prove the change history was
not rewritten after the fact.

### Ten-year retention, and a real diff

- Each revision carries a **10-year `retention_until`** (`GPAI_ART53_RETENTION_YEARS`),
  computed from its `created_at` — Art. 53's retention semantics, on the artifact itself.
- `diff_revisions(older, newer)` produces a field-level diff — `added`, `removed`,
  `modified` — so a material change is not just recorded but *legible*. Unchanged fields
  are not reported.

### Scope, honestly

A pure-code, offline exporter — no infrastructure, no new dependencies, reusing the shared
hash utility. With NF-093, **every pure-code exporter in ADR-0107 is now implemented**:
NF-090 (Art. 12, v0.80.0), NF-091 (Art. 72 PMM, v0.85.0), NF-093 (this release), NF-094
(Art. 50 marking, v0.86.0), NF-095 (ISO 42001/42005, v0.84.0); NF-092 (Annex IV) is served
by the shipped `AnnexIVExporter`. Only **NF-097** (NIST GenAI / CSA Agentic mapper) remains
future design. ADR-0107 §NF-093 marked shipped under delegated authority.
