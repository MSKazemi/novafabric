# v0.12.6 — Asset Unregister (AI-7)

Released: 2026-05-14

Closes the final asset lifecycle gap: safe hard-deletion of registry entries with a
status guard, lineage cleanup, and a full audit trail. No schema changes; purely
additive.

## What's new

### nova unregister \<name@version\>

Hard-deletes an asset version from the registry. Removes the asset row, associated
eval results, approvals, and synthetic dependency edges. Preserves all consumed and
`depends_on` edges — they capture real run provenance.

Status guard: `staging`, `production`, and `pending_approval` assets are blocked
unless `--force` is passed. `development`, `validated`, and `archived` assets may be
deleted without `--force`.

```bash
nova unregister fraud-model@1.0.0
nova unregister broken-agent@dev --force --actor ops-eng
nova unregister old-model@v2 --yes           # CI-friendly (no prompt)
```

Options:
- `--actor <id>` — identity in the audit trail (defaults to OS username).
- `--force` — override the status guard; permits deletion of active-lifecycle assets.
- `--yes, -y` — skip interactive confirmation.
- `--db <path>` — override registry DB path (`NOVAFABRIC_DB_PATH` env var also accepted).

Always writes an `UNREGISTER` audit entry with actor, status at deletion, and asset ID.
