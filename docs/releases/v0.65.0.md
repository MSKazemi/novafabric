# NovaFabric v0.65.0 — 2026-07-24

The **compliance-provenance release**: the first slice of ADR-0197 gives
NovaFabric's compliance exports an honest answer to the question that matters
most in a regulated context — *who established this fact?* Everything new is
`experimental` and additive. Full detail: `CHANGELOG.md` §[Unreleased].

## Highlights

### `evidence_source` provenance marker (ADR-0197)

A 2026-07-20 audit found that the shipped compliance-export layer emitted
evidence-shaped documents whose provenance a consumer could not tell apart: a
value NovaFabric verified against a sealed capsule looked identical to a value
an operator typed into JSON. For a 21 CFR Part 11 record, an SR 11-7 report, a
GDPR Article 15 DSAR, or a FOIA disclosure, *who established the fact* is the
entire product.

This release adds an **`evidence_source`** marker to every field-group of the
field-group–structured exporters:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `operator_asserted` | The value came from operator-authored input; NovaFabric did not check it. |
| `capsule_verified` | NovaFabric resolved this from a capsule and re-performed the binding. Carries an `evidence_ref` (`capsule_id` + `content_digest` [+ optional `seal_envelope_path`]) a third party can re-check offline. |
| `unverifiable` | NovaFabric attempted verification and could not complete it — reported, never downgraded to an assertion. |

- **Shared primitive** — `novafabric.compliance.export.provenance`:
  the `EvidenceSource` enum, the re-performable `EvidenceSourceRef`, `mark()`
  (enforces that `capsule_verified` carries a reference and the others do not),
  `build_capsule_ref()`, and `validate_marked()` (export-time I-1/I-3 enforcement).
- **Wired into** `export-annex-iv`, `export-nis2`, and the incident-store AIM /
  DORA projections. `capsule_verified` entries carry a `sha256:` digest over the
  capsule files a third party can re-hash; cap-006-gated NIS2 fields are
  `unverifiable`, never `operator_asserted`; the Annex IV renderer surfaces the
  marker.
- **Additive and optional on the wire** — a pre-ADR-0197 document deserializes
  with `evidence_source: null`, so nothing external breaks; every shipped
  field-group exporter now populates it.

**Scope, honestly.** This is the first slice. The pure-projection
sector/transparency renderers (SR 11-7, Part 11, FOIA, …) adopt the marker in a
later slice, and the fully-required envelope-v2 flip (no default) is deferred
until every family is migrated — see
`design/spec/evidence-source-provenance-marker.md` for both decisions and their
rationale.

### OpenAPI robustness

The two dashboard file-download routes — `/api/runs/{run_id}/file/{filepath}`
and `/api/evidence/{bundle_id}/download` — now declare `response_model=None`,
matching the codebase's guard convention for `Response`-returning routes.

## Upgrade notes

No breaking changes. The marker is additive and optional; existing exports keep
working and gain provenance marking automatically. No new dependencies.
