# NovaFabric v0.85.0 — 2026-07-25

**Post-market monitoring that escalates onto the incident clock you already have.**
ADR-0107's Art. 72 PMM generator (NF-091) ships: compile a post-market-monitoring
report from your monitoring findings, and let a serious finding open a *referred*
incident that the existing Art. 73 deadline clock governs — one clock, not two.
`experimental`. Full detail: `CHANGELOG.md` §[0.85.0].

## Highlights

### The Art. 72 PMM generator (ADR-0107 §NF-091)

`build_pmm_report(system_name, period_start=…, period_end=…, findings=…, occurred_at=…)`
compiles a post-market-monitoring report from monitoring findings — each a `metric`, a
`trend` (improving / stable / degrading), a `severity`, and a description, optionally
tagged with the run ids that surfaced it. Every finding is carried in the report.

### It escalates onto the shipped incident clock — it does not duplicate it

The EU AI Act wires Art. 72 (post-market monitoring) into Art. 73 (serious-incident
reporting): a monitoring finding that turns out to be a serious incident inherits Art. 73's
hard deadlines. NovaFabric already ships that clock (ADR-0088: `Incident` + `DeadlineClock`).
So NF-091 **reuses** it:

- A finding whose severity crosses the **serious-incident threshold**
  (`is_serious` — `CRITICAL` or `HIGH`) produces a *referred* `Incident`, built from the
  **shipped ADR-0088 model**, anchored at the report's `occurred_at`.
- The caller persists each referred incident via the existing `IncidentStore`, and the
  existing `DeadlineClock.compute(incident, now)` produces its Art. 73 obligation
  deadlines. No second clock, no divergent deadline logic.

A serious finding **must** carry an `incident_classification` (the cap-005 taxonomy the
clock reads to pick the 15-/10-/2-day obligation). One without it **raises** — you cannot
open an Art. 73 incident without classifying it, and silently dropping the escalation would
be worse than failing. Fail closed.

### Scope, honestly

A pure projection over monitoring findings plus a referral into existing incident
machinery — no infrastructure, no new dependencies. This is the **third** ADR-0107
exporter (NF-090 Art. 12 in v0.80.0, NF-095 ISO 42001/42005 in v0.84.0; NF-092 Annex IV is
served by the shipped `AnnexIVExporter`). The remaining three — NF-093 (GPAI Art. 53 form),
NF-094 (Art. 50 marking log), NF-097 (NIST GenAI / CSA mapper) — remain future design. A
`nova export-*` CLI wrapper is a deliberate follow-on. ADR-0107 §NF-091 marked shipped
under delegated authority.
