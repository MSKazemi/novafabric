# NovaFabric v0.86.0 — 2026-07-25

**Prove you disclosed AI content — two ways, one receipt.** ADR-0107's Art. 50 marking
log (NF-094) ships: log every AI-disclosure event, and carry a SynthID-presence
assertion *inside* the C2PA manifest NovaFabric already emits — recorded and verified,
never faked. `experimental`. Full detail: `CHANGELOG.md` §[0.86.0].

## Highlights

### The Art. 50 marking log (ADR-0107 §NF-094)

Article 50 requires providers to disclose that content is AI-generated.
`build_marking_log(events)` records each marking/disclosure event — the `content_id`, the
`methods` used (`c2pa_manifest`, `synthid_watermark`, `visible_disclosure`), what it was
`marked_as`, when, and the originating `run_id` — as sealed-ready evidence. A marking with
no method raises: a disclosure with no method is not a disclosure.

### A dual-layer provenance receipt — built on the C2PA manifest you already emit

NovaFabric has emitted C2PA Content Credentials since ADR-0074. NF-094 adds a **second
layer** *inside* that same manifest:

- `attach_synthid_presence(manifest, present=…, detector=…, verified_at=…)` injects a
  **SynthID-presence assertion** into the shipped C2PA manifest — non-mutating (it returns
  a copy), appended to the active manifest's assertions so it travels with the credential.
- `verify_synthid_assertion(manifest)` reads it back. That read-back *is* NovaFabric's
  verification of the C2PA assertion.
- `build_dual_layer_receipt(...)` bundles layer 1 (the enriched C2PA manifest) and layer 2
  (the Art. 50 marking log) into one `DualLayerReceipt`.

### Verify-only, by design

SynthID is Google-proprietary. NovaFabric **records and verifies** a presence claim from
an external detector — it **never generates or embeds a SynthID watermark**. The assertion
itself carries that note, so a downstream reader can never mistake the receipt for
NovaFabric having produced a watermark. This is the honest boundary ADR-0107 §NF-094 draws.

### Scope, honestly

A pure projection plus a non-mutating enrichment of an existing manifest — no
infrastructure, no new dependencies, and a real integration with the shipped
`C2PAManifestExporter` (the tests attach to and verify a manifest built by the actual
ADR-0074 exporter). This is the **fourth** ADR-0107 exporter (NF-090 Art. 12 v0.80.0,
NF-095 ISO 42001/42005 v0.84.0, NF-091 Art. 72 PMM v0.85.0; NF-092 Annex IV served by the
shipped `AnnexIVExporter`). The remaining two — NF-093 (GPAI Art. 53 form) and NF-097
(NIST GenAI / CSA mapper) — remain future design. ADR-0107 §NF-094 marked shipped under
delegated authority.
