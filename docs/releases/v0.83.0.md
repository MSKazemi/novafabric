# NovaFabric v0.83.0 — 2026-07-25

**Where your logs live is now cryptographically provable.** ADR-0077's first slice
ships jurisdiction *site-seals* — a countersignature that proves a capsule really was
sealed in the region it claims — plus a residency policy that blocks cross-border reads
unless you explicitly grant them. `experimental`. Full detail: `CHANGELOG.md` §[0.83.0].

## Highlights

### Cryptographic proof of residency (ADR-0077 §3)

Data-residency regimes (GDPR, the RBI AI framework, NFRA guidelines) increasingly demand
that AI logs stay in their region of origin. A *claim* of residency in metadata is not
proof — it can be edited. A **site-seal** is:

- `issue_site_seal(jurisdiction_key, jurisdiction="EU", content_digest=…)` mints an
  Ed25519 countersignature over a domain-separated `(jurisdiction, content_digest)`
  tuple, made by that region's site-seal key.
- `verify_site_seal(seal, jurisdiction_pubkeys=…)` verifies the signature under the key
  **registered for the jurisdiction the seal claims**. A capsule that claims `EU` but was
  sealed by a US key does not verify against the real EU key — it is rejected. So is a
  tampered digest or an unregistered jurisdiction.

The whole check is offline: no per-region infrastructure is needed to *verify* residency,
only to *produce* seals at each site.

### Residency-respecting reads (ADR-0077 §2)

- `ResidencyPolicy(allow_cross_read={"EU": ["CH"]})` declares which reader jurisdictions
  may read which data jurisdictions. The grant is **directional** — EU letting CH read EU
  data does not let EU read CH data.
- `check_cross_jurisdiction_read(capsule_jurisdiction, reader_jurisdiction, policy)`
  returns a `ReadDecision(allowed, cross_jurisdiction, reason)`. Same-jurisdiction reads
  are always allowed; cross-border reads are denied by default.

This is the storage-abstraction gate of ADR-0077 §2, shipped as a pure decision function
the routing layer will call.

### Scope, honestly

This ships the **verifiable half** — the seal format, its verifier, and the residency
policy. The **infrastructure half remains future design**: the `jurisdiction` capture-time
metadata field, per-jurisdiction storage-backend routing (which needs live per-region
Postgres/S3 backends), and the jurisdiction-scoped lineage traversal filter. All of them
consume this format and policy with **no format change** — shipping the verifier now is
what makes them additive later. ADR-0077 moved `Future Design → Accepted` for this slice
under delegated authority.
